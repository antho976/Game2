# Story working notes

These notes preserve the author's initial premise. They are not a finished script or a replacement for the author's story. Unresolved details remain open.

## Premise

Gods secretly shape civilizations and manipulate them into wars. They implanted greed in humanity to encourage the conflict through which they harvest the nourishment of life and death.

After thousands of years, Earth's output no longer satisfies them. The planet enters a harvesting phase: the termination of all life when its civilization fails to produce enough.

A fallen god has taken refuge on Earth and found love. He comes to understand how deeply humans attach themselves to their lives and the little things within them. When Earth's destruction approaches, he initially sees two choices: take the woman he loves away, or let her die.

He finds a third option. He sacrifices himself to create portals to other planets, giving Earth a chance to survive by harvesting those worlds.

## Hidden lore: not initial player-facing exposition

- The fallen god fled to Earth because it was away from the gods' eyes. Gods who cannot produce enough nourishment through planetary exploitation are cast into the abyss; he escaped that fate and took refuge here.
- The player is a lost child of a god, raised among humans. They gradually discover their difference and what their kind does.
- The eventual choices under consideration are to end the cycle, preserve it and join the gods, or continue harvesting other planets to keep Earth alive.

## Causal details still to define

These are design questions, not decisions:

- What does harvesting a planet involve, and what happens to its inhabitants?
- How does harvesting another world protect Earth from its own termination?
- What changes allowed a world used as a refuge away from the gods' eyes to become subject to their harvesting decision?

The first hub test does not need to answer or reveal all of these. It should remain compatible with the author's later answers.
